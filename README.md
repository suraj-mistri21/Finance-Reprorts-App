This application is used for generating, validating, and maintaining business reports from Shopify and related data sources.

The tool currently supports report development and updates for areas such as:
- Order and transaction reporting
- Payment and refund reporting
- Partner and location master reports
- Product and catalog filtering
- Entity and location-level information
- Shopify order, payment, refund, and transaction details
- Custom metafield-based data such as Register Entity Name
- Data validation and troubleshooting of missing or incorrect records

The application is primarily used to develop and maintain Python-based reporting logic, investigate data discrepancies, and ensure that the generated reports contain the required business fields and records accurately.

It also helps identify issues such as missing orders/refunds, incorrect product filtering, incorrect transaction references, and mismatches between Shopify data and the generated reports.
